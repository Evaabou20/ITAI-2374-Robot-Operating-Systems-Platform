# Robotics Course Portfolio

This portfolio was created as the final examination for **ITAI 2374 – Robotics and AI Platforms** at Houston City College. Rather than presenting a single project, it documents my learning journey throughout the semester by highlighting the major assignments, laboratory activities, presentations, and reflections completed during the course.

The portfolio demonstrates the progression of my knowledge in robotics, embedded systems, artificial intelligence, industrial automation, and collaborative robotics.
